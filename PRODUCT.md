# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineering teams, consultants, Document Controllers, and executive leadership at a Dubai engineering consultancy operating in both office environments and on active construction sites:

- **Discipline Engineers (Authority, MEP, Architect, Structure, Civil):** In office or on-site, tracking drawing revisions, editing technical submittals, and updating discipline-specific status for assigned projects.
- **Resident Engineers (RE) & Area Managers (AM):** Reviewing submittals, verifying drawings against authority requirements, providing formal sign-offs/rejections with required comments, and tracking client contract milestones.
- **Document Controllers (DC):** Managing project document archives, overseeing file integrity, dispatching authority submittals, and monitoring authority reference numbers.
- **Administrators & Executive Leadership (CEO, Area Managers):** Monitoring firm-wide project portfolios, financial contract status, approval bottlenecks, and authority compliance across Dubai jurisdictions (Nakheel/Trakhees, Dubai Municipality, DDA).

## Product Purpose

A centralized, high-reliability engineering document control and authority NOC tracking platform replacing chaotic SharePoint folder trees and error-prone Excel masterlogs. The platform guarantees:

- Strict role-based and project-assignment-based access control protecting confidential commercial data while keeping engineers focused on their projects.
- Comprehensive tracking of the full authority NOC lifecycle (submission, reviewing authority dependencies, blocking sequences, fee payments, issuance, and automated 7-day expiry warnings).
- Uncompromising auditability for every drawing submission, revision (`R00`, `R01`, ...), rejection comment, and dual-level approval (RE and AM).

## Brand Personality

- **Industrial Precision:** Rigorous, dependable, utilitarian, and fast.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, transparent, focused, and distraction-free.
- **Emotional Target:** Unshakable confidence in compliance deadlines, certainty in document versions, and operational efficiency under pressure.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS Marketing Fluff:** No oversized rounded cards (border-radius > 16px), decorative gradient text, gratuitous illustration, or bloated white space.
- **Legacy ERP Spaghetti:** No unstyled 1990s table sprawl, nested hidden menus, or cryptic error codes.
- **Consumer App Casualness:** No pastel badges, playful micro-copy, or chat-app gimmicks.
- **Slow Animated Choreography:** No multi-step page entrance animations or delayed transitions that interrupt technical workflows.

## Design Principles

- **Zero-Friction Compliance:** Every submittal, revision, and approval creates an effortless, permanent audit trail without burdensome administrative overhead.
- **Urgency Through Clarity:** Pending submittals, days of delay, and NOC expiration timers stand out with unmistakable contrast and immediate action pathways.
- **Dual-Axis Privacy:** Role-based and project-assignment filters guarantee that confidential finances remain protected while engineers operate with least privilege.
- **Earned Familiarity:** Embrace dense, scannable data layouts, standard tabular controls, and predictable keyboard shortcuts reminiscent of the best technical developer and CAD utilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance:** Minimum 4.5:1 contrast for all text against backgrounds and 3:1 for graphical UI elements; engineered for bright daylight readability on mobile PWA screens.
- **Keyboard First:** Full interactive support for tabular navigation, dialog dismissal, and fast batch document operations.
- **Reduced Motion:** Instant state changes when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is detected.
- **Adaptive Density:** High-information-density layouts on desktop multi-monitors that collapse gracefully into clear touch-friendly inspector cards on tablet/mobile screens.
