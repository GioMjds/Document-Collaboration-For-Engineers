# Business Brief Info - CVTEC Consulting Engineers

## What CVTEC is

**CVTEC Consulting Engineers** is a **Dubai-based multidisciplinary architecture, engineering, and construction consultancy**. In simple terms, they help clients take construction and development projects from an initial concept through engineering/design and ultimately construction supervision. ([CVTEC][1])

They have been operating since **2005** and report **291+ delivered projects** and approximately **$4.2 billion in built value managed**. ([CVTEC][2])

### Their main areas

| Area                   | What they do                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Architecture**       | Architectural design, master planning, urban planning, landscape, interiors, BIM |
| **Engineering**        | Structural, MEP, infrastructure, geotechnical, marine engineering                |
| **Construction**       | Site supervision, contract administration, project/construction management       |
| **Design Consulting**  | Feasibility studies, value engineering, technical advisory                       |
| **Project Management** | Development management, design management, financial feasibility                 |

([CVTEC][3])

### They work on substantial construction projects

Their portfolio includes things such as:

- Luxury hotels
- Residential towers and villas
- Mixed-use developments
- Shopping/retail centers
- Healthcare and institutional buildings
- Infrastructure
- Marine/coastal projects
- Master-planned developments

For example, the website lists projects including **Conrad Palm Jumeirah**, **Premier Inn Hotel**, **Four Seasons Hotel Beirut**, **Galleria Barsha Retail Centre**, and **RA1N Residence**. ([CVTEC][2])

### BIM is also a significant part of their workflow

They emphasize **Building Information Modeling (BIM)**, describing architectural, structural, and MEP models being federated into a unified model. Their stated goal is to use BIM as a coordinated source of information from design through fabrication/construction. ([CVTEC][1])

### Their workflow

Their overall project lifecycle can basically be understood as:

### **1. Feasibility → 2. Design → 3. Construction → 4. Handover**

They handle things such as site studies and feasibility, detailed engineering/design, documentation, procurement, construction management, inspection, and commissioning. ([CVTEC][2])

### In one sentence

> **CVTEC is essentially a full-service engineering and construction consultancy that helps property developers and clients design, engineer, manage, and deliver buildings and infrastructure projects.**
